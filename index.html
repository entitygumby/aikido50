50 Years of Aikido in Australia

This seminar marks a significant milestone in Australian martial arts history - the 50th anniversary of Takayasu Sensei's teaching of Aikido in Australia.

Over five decades, Takayasu Sensei has tirelessly worked to spread the art of Aikido, training countless students and establishing a strong foundation for the practice throughout the country.

This celebration brings together the Australian Aikido community to honour this remarkable achievement and to continue learning from Sensei's wealth of knowledge and experience.

Seminar Highlights

What you'll experience over the weekend

🥋

Aikido Kihon

Tai-no-henko, Ki-no-nagare, Suwari-waza from Ikkyo to Gokyo. Understanding Awase-no-kokyu (resonance action).

🔄

Throwing Techniques

Shiho-nage, Kote-gaeshi, Irimi-nage, Kokyu-nage, Koshi-nage, Tenchi-nage, and Juji-garami-nage.

⚔️

Ken Techniques

100 cuts suburi, 7 Ken Awase, Kimusubi-no-Tachi, 5 Kumitachi and variations.

📍

Jo Techniques

31 Jo-kata, 13 Jo-kata, 7 Jo-awase, 10 Kumi-jo, and 31 Jo-awase.

🎭

Demonstrations

Watch demonstrations by Aikido instructors and related Japanese martial arts instructors (Kenjutsu, Jujitsu).

🍽️

Anniversary Dinner

Join us Saturday evening for a special 50th anniversary celebration dinner.
