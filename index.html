The Way of Harmony

Aikido, "The Art of Peace," was founded by Morihei Ueshiba (O-Sensei) in the early 20th century. More than just a martial art, Aikido is a path of personal development that emphasizes harmony, non-resistance, and the peaceful resolution of conflict.

Through dedicated practice, we learn to blend with and redirect aggressive energy rather than meeting it with force. This principle extends beyond the dojo into daily life, helping us navigate challenges with grace and composure.

This seminar celebrates 50 years of our organization's commitment to preserving and sharing O-Sensei's vision with practitioners worldwide.
