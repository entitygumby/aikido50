Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the seminar

What experience level is required?

+

All experience levels are welcome. Takayasu Sensei will provide instruction suitable for both beginners and advanced practitioners. Weapons experience is helpful for Day 2 but not required.

Do I need to bring my own weapons?

+

Yes, please bring your own bokken (wooden sword) and jo (wooden staff) for the Sunday weapons training sessions. If you don't have weapons, please contact the organisers in advance.

Can I attend just one day?

+

Yes, single-day registration options are available. However, we encourage attending both days for the complete experience, especially the Saturday dinner celebration.

Is the dinner included in registration?

+

The anniversary dinner on Saturday evening may have a separate fee. Please check the registration page for current pricing and options, or visit aikidoaus.com.au for details.

What should I wear to the dinner?

+

Smart casual attire is appropriate for the 50th anniversary celebration dinner. No need for formal wear, but please dress respectfully for this special occasion.
