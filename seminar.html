Our Distinguished Instructors

Learn from masters who have dedicated their lives to Aikido

🥋

Sensei Takeshi Yamamoto

7th Dan Shihan

Born in Tokyo, Sensei Yamamoto began his Aikido journey at age 15, training directly under several of O-Sensei's most senior students at Hombu Dojo. With over 45 years of experience, he is renowned for his precise technique and deep understanding of Aikido's spiritual foundations. Currently the chief instructor at the International Aikido Academy.

🥋

Sensei Maria Santos

6th Dan Shihan

A pioneer of Aikido in South America, Sensei Santos has been instrumental in spreading the art across the continent for over 35 years. Known for her expertise in weapons training (bukiwaza) and her innovative teaching methodology, she has trained thousands of students and established numerous dojos throughout Brazil and Argentina.

🥋

Sensei Robert Chen

6th Dan Shihan

With a background in multiple martial arts before discovering Aikido 30 years ago, Sensei Chen brings a unique perspective to his teaching. His dynamic style and ability to explain complex concepts in accessible terms has made him a favorite at international seminars. He leads the Aikido Society of North America.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the seminar

What experience level is required?

+

All experience levels are welcome, from complete beginners to advanced practitioners. Instructors will provide variations suitable for different skill levels during each session.

Do I need to attend all sessions?

+

While we encourage full participation, we understand that personal circumstances may vary. You are welcome to rest during sessions if needed, but the full experience is highly recommended.

Is there accommodation nearby?

+

Yes! We have partnered with several hotels within walking distance of the venue. Registered participants will receive a discount code for these partner hotels.

Are meals included?

+

Yes, lunch is provided on all three days, plus dinner on Friday evening and a farewell lunch on Sunday. Please indicate any dietary restrictions during registration.

Will there be testing during the seminar?

+

There will be optional testing available for kyu ranks on Sunday morning. Dan examinations require pre-registration and eligibility verification through your home dojo.

What is the refund policy?

+

Full refunds are available up to 30 days before the event. 50% refund available 15-30 days before. No refunds within 14 days of the event, but registration can be transferred to another participant.
