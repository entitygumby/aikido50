Important Notes

Cancellation Policy

+

Full refund: Cancellations made 30+ days before the event.
50% refund: Cancellations made 15-30 days before the event.
No refund: Cancellations within 14 days of the event. However, you may transfer your registration to another participant.
All refund requests must be submitted in writing to info@aikido50seminar.com

Confirmation Process

+

Upon successful payment, you will receive a confirmation email within 24 hours containing your registration details, seminar schedule, and practical information. Please save this email and bring it with you to the seminar for check-in.

Student Discount Eligibility

+

The student discount is available to full-time students with a valid student ID. You will be required to present your student ID at registration. If you cannot provide proof of student status, you will be asked to pay the difference for the full seminar pass.

Group Discounts

+

Groups of 5 or more from the same dojo receive a 10% discount. Groups of 10 or more receive a 15% discount. Contact us directly at info@aikido50seminar.com to arrange group registration.
