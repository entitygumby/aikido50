Important Information

What to Bring - Saturday (Empty Hand)

+

Clean white keikogi (Aikido uniform). Hakama for yudansha (black belt holders). Bring a water bottle and any personal first aid items you may need.

What to Bring - Sunday (Weapons)

+

Essential: Bokken (wooden sword) and Jo (wooden staff). Please bring your own weapons. If you don't have weapons, contact the organisers in advance to arrange.

Anniversary Dinner

+

The 50th anniversary celebration dinner is on Saturday evening at 6:30pm. This is a wonderful opportunity to celebrate with Takayasu Sensei and the Aikido community. Smart casual attire is appropriate.

Payment & Confirmation

+

For payment details and to confirm your registration, please visit aikidoaus.com.au or contact Aikido Australia directly.
