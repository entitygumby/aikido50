Quick Answers

Common questions about the seminar

What experience level is required?

+

All experience levels are welcome, from beginners to advanced practitioners. Takayasu Sensei will provide instruction suitable for everyone.

Do I need weapons for Sunday?

+

Yes, please bring your own bokken (wooden sword) and jo (wooden staff) for the Sunday weapons sessions. If you don't have weapons, please contact the organisers in advance.

Can I attend just one day?

+

Yes, single-day options are available. However, we encourage attending both days for the complete experience.

Where can I find pricing information?

+

For current pricing and registration details, please visit aikidoaus.com.au.

What should I wear to the anniversary dinner?

+

Smart casual attire is appropriate for the 50th anniversary celebration dinner on Saturday evening.
