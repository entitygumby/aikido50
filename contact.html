Quick Answers

Common questions we receive

What are your office hours?

+

Our office is open Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM Japan Standard Time (JST). We check emails over the weekend but may not respond until Monday.

How quickly will you respond to my inquiry?

+

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24-48 business hours. During peak registration periods, responses may take slightly longer. For urgent matters, please call our phone line.

I haven't received my registration confirmation. What should I do?

+

First, please check your spam/junk folder. If you still can't find it, email us at info@aikido50seminar.com with your full name and the email used for registration, and we'll resend your confirmation.

Can I visit the venue before the seminar?

+

The Tokyo Convention Center is open to the public, and you're welcome to visit the general areas. However, the specific training hall will only be accessible during the seminar.
